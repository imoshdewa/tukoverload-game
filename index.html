Pile it high. Drive it home.

A wobbly, giggly driving game made for little hands. No reading needed: everything is pictures and big buttons.

🧸

Stack it

Teddies, cakes, ice cream and presents. Pile them up your way, and tie the wobbly ones down with a rope.

🛺

Drive it

Hold to go, press the big red brake to stop. Every bump makes the cargo wobble!

🐄

Watch out

Cows wander onto the road, cars pull out and lights turn red. Slow down and wait!

⭐

Earn stars

Up to three stars on every level, a best score to beat, and new maps to open.

Three bright worlds

The countryside, a busy neighbourhood and the big city, each with its own road to the drop-off.

Every December: a free Christmas world

From 1 December to 6 January the tuk-tuk turns into Santa's sleigh!

  • 🎁 Tap the children by the road to toss them a present. Slow down so it lands!
  • 🏠 Drop gifts down the chimneys for a bonus.
  • 🧝 Stop at the elves' workshop for more presents.
  • 🦌 On Christmas Eve, Rudolph's red nose lights the way.
